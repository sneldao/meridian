# Pitch narrative — verified framing material

Cold-open line: **"The people who beat jet lag for a living have a crew. We
built yours."**

## Verified anchors (source before stage)

- **Nico Rosberg, F1 World Champion** — his own LinkedIn post: shifted sleep
  "a maximum of 1.5 hours a day" pre/post travel, blackout glasses before
  bed, 10,000 lux lamp at wake-up, no screens 1.5h before bed ("that stops
  the body's melatonin production"). *This is our scorer's rulebook:* 90
  min/day shift ceiling, morning light anchor, pre-bed light avoidance.
  Stage line: "Our deterministic gate encodes what a world champion does by
  hand."
- **McLaren F1** — official site: sleep is "meticulously managed and
  measured," jet lag "a consistent thorn in the side of every team" across
  a 24-race, 21-country calendar.
- **The Rock** — GQ: exercise "drives your system to adjust to the local
  time zones, combats jet lag and helps anchor you." (Exercise timing is an
  intervention kind in our plan schema.)
- **Nick Littlehales** — the original elite sport sleep coach; coached
  Ronaldo and Beckham; CNN-covered Nov 2025. Proof that "crew for your
  body clock" is a real job — just only affordable to elite sport.
- **Dawson & Reid 1997** — 24h awake ≈ BAC 0.10 reaction impairment.
  The performance-cost receipt.

## 3-minute arc

1. **Hook** — Rosberg's routine read aloud; "this is a staff job in F1."
2. **Persona** — Alex (LHR→HND, Garmin, calendar-driven, ignores pre-7am
   asks). Judges know this person — or are this person.
3. **Live run** — crew debates, evaluator gates a plan (real rejection →
   replan on screen), real calendar writes.
4. **The insight** — Valentina interview: "wouldn't pay for advice,
   wouldn't adhere" — so the scorer penalizes plans she won't follow.
5. **Land** — "Elite athletes get a crew. Everyone else gets an app full of
   advice they ignore. Meridian is the crew."
